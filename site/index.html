About the academy

What Excellence English Academy is producing

One teacher, one curriculum, one clear path from a child's first sentence to exam-ready fluency.

Nazar ElHaj teaches English grammar and language skills to students in Grades 1–11, using the SMILE Series as the core curriculum.

Lessons are shared across WhatsApp, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram and Instagram, so students can review class material, watch explanations again, and practice between sessions — not just during them.

This website brings all of that together in one place: what each grade covers, and every video, audio lesson, worksheet image and exam paper available to download.

  • Grade-by-grade grammar

    A structured path through grammar and language skills for every grade from 1 to 11, built on the SMILE Series.

  • Video lessons

    Recorded explanations students can rewatch and download, matched to the grade and topic they need.

  • Audio lessons

    Listening and pronunciation practice as downloadable audio, for study on the go.

  • Worksheets, images & exam papers

    Printable pages, reference images and past exams, ready to download and use for revision.
